Privacy Policy

Factory Ledger · CNS Confectionery Products LLC

Effective September 16, 2026

Factory Ledger is an internal manufacturing operations tool owned and used solely by CNS Confectionery Products LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company (“CNS,” “we,” or “us”). This policy explains what data the tool handles and how we treat it.

1. Single-Company Internal Use

Factory Ledger is not a public or commercial product. It is used only by CNS employees and contractors to run CNS’s own factory operations. There are no third-party users, no customer accounts, and no public sign-up. We do not collect information from the general public through this tool, and these pages themselves set no cookies and run no scripts or analytics.

2. What Data the Tool Handles

  • Operational records: products, lots, inventory movements, production runs, sales orders, purchase orders, and related manufacturing activity.
  • QuickBooks Online data: read-only access to purchase order information — such as vendor names, purchase order numbers and dates, line items, quantities, and amounts — used only to sync purchase orders into Factory Ledger.
  • Internal user identifiers: the name or account of the CNS user who recorded an action, kept for the audit trail.

3. QuickBooks Online

We connect to QuickBooks Online with credentials authorized by CNS for its own company file. That connection is read only: Factory Ledger reads purchase order data and does not write to, modify, or delete anything in QuickBooks Online. The data we read is used for the single purpose of keeping purchase orders in sync with our operational records. CNS can disconnect the integration at any time from within QuickBooks Online or by removing the connection in Factory Ledger.

4. Where Data Is Stored

Data synced from QuickBooks Online, along with all other Factory Ledger records, is stored in our own database, hosted by our infrastructure providers for the sole purpose of operating this tool. Access is limited to authorized CNS personnel and protected by authentication and encrypted connections.

5. What We Never Do

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share this data with any third party. We do not use it for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose other than running CNS’s own operations. Data is disclosed outside CNS only if required by law or to our own service providers strictly as needed to host and maintain the system.

6. Retention

Because Factory Ledger is a manufacturing and traceability record, operational data is retained as a business record for as long as CNS needs it for production, inventory, accounting, and food-traceability purposes. Data pulled from QuickBooks Online is deleted or retired when it is no longer needed for those purposes.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The version posted at this address is the current one, and the effective date above reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about data held in Factory Ledger, may be sent by email to miriam@cnscoinc.com.
